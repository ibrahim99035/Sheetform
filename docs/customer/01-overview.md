# SiroQ at a glance

> Non-technical summary for the customer conversation. Companion diagram: `01-overview.drawio`.

## Who this system is built for

**You are the customer.** You run a pharmacy association (or a similar
organization that serves pharmacies). SiroQ is the system **you** operate to
deliver a data-advisory service to **your** client pharmacies.

**Your client pharmacies are the end beneficiaries.** They provide their data;
SiroQ turns it into monthly advice that each pharmacy can act on. You provide
and run the service; the value each pharmacy gets is the outcome of it.

## The three-part picture

| Part | Who | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your pharmacies** | Your clients | They send the files they already keep (Excel/CSV), month by month. Each pharmacy only ever sees its own data. |
| **You (the operator)** | The association | One dashboard for all your pharmacies: collect, analyze, and publish advice per pharmacy. |
| **Back to your pharmacies** | The result | Advice is delivered by email and/or WhatsApp, and stays available 24/7 in each pharmacy's account. |

## What you get in practice

1. **One place for everything** — every pharmacy, every file, every report, every
   historical record, in a single dashboard.
2. **Analysis per pharmacy** — totals, quantities, costs, margins, and month-on-month
   comparisons, computed automatically for each branch.
3. **A service you can deliver** — advice is drafted and published per pharmacy
   with minimal manual work, so the service is repeatable month after month.
4. **Delivery** — reports go out automatically by email and/or WhatsApp and are
   always available in the pharmacy's own account.
5. **A clean record** — everything is kept, ordered, and auditable, so you can
   show precisely what value you have delivered.

## Who is on which side of the deal

- **SiroQ** is the software platform (this product).
- **You (the association)** buy and operate it — you are our customer.
- **Your client pharmacies** are *your* customers; they benefit from the advice,
   and you manage their service on top of SiroQ.
