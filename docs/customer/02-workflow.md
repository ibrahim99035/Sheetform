# How SiroQ works — the monthly rhythm of your service

> Plain-language walkthrough for the customer conversation. Companion diagram: `02-workflow.drawio`.
> "Your pharmacies" below means the client pharmacies you serve.

## The seven steps

| Step | Who | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. A pharmacy sends you its data** | The pharmacy | They send the Excel/CSV files they already keep — nothing to retype for anyone. |
| **2. SiroQ checks and imports it** | SiroQ | Columns and types are recognized automatically; each file is stored privately, per pharmacy. |
| **3. SiroQ analyzes the numbers** | SiroQ | Totals, quantities, costs, margins — for each pharmacy, and compared over time. |
| **4. You review and publish advice** | You | A readable report per pharmacy, ready for you to review and approve. |
| **5. You publish the report** | You | The report lives in the pharmacy's account, safe, always reachable, with full history. |
| **6. It reaches the pharmacy its way** | SiroQ | Automatic delivery by email and/or WhatsApp — whichever each pharmacy prefers. |
| **7. They act — you grow the service** | Both | The pharmacy makes better decisions; you keep a clean record of the value you deliver. |

## What this means for you (the operator)

- **Step 4** is where *you* add value. SiroQ prepares everything; you review and
  approve what goes out under your service's name.
- **Steps 2, 3, 5, 6** run with almost no manual effort — which is what turns a
  one-off favour into a **repeatable monthly service**.
- **Every month, every pharmacy, same rhythm** — the system is designed around
  serving many pharmacies at once from a single operator account.

## What this means for your pharmacies

- They only ever hand over files they already produce (step 1).
- They never have to analyze anything — they receive the finished advice
  (steps 4–6).
- They make better decisions from their own real numbers (step 7).

## The colours in the diagram

- **Pink** boxes = the pharmacy (your client).
- **Blue** boxes = SiroQ together with you, the operator.
- **Green** boxes = the result and how it reaches the pharmacy.

That's the whole service: **pharmacies send data once; you run a monthly advice
service on top; they act on clear advice.**
