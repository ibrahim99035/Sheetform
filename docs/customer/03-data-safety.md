# Your pharmacies' data — safe, separate, and on the record

> Plain-language privacy explanation for the customer conversation. Companion diagram: `03-data-safety.drawio`.
> "Your pharmacies" below means the client pharmacies you serve.

## Three promises

### 1. Privacy from the start

- Each pharmacy can only see its own data and reports — nothing leaks sideways.
- Access is by account and password on every device.
- Associations and pharmacies are kept fully isolated from each other.
- Sensitive data (finance, health-adjacent records) is handled with extra care.

### 2. You stay in control

Running the service means fielding questions about data — and SiroQ answers them:

- **Copy** — when a pharmacy asks for a copy of its data, you can provide it.
- **Delete** — when a pharmacy asks for its data to be removed, the system supports it.
- **Retention** — data is kept only as long as needed, then cleaned up automatically
  (sales/financial data is kept for a bounded period; health-related data for a
  longer one, as the regulations expect).
- **Terms** — the rules your service needs are clear, readable, and accepted before
  anything starts. You define and enforce them.

### 3. On the record

- Every important action (who did what, and when) is recorded automatically.
- That journal **cannot be erased** — protection for you as much as for your pharmacies.
- It is a real, monitored system run by a real team.

## What this means in everyday language

Your pharmacies' numbers are commercially sensitive — and sometimes about people.
SiroQ treats both seriously:

- You never hand data into a black box: everything each pharmacy can know about
  itself is visible, and deletion requests are supported.
- Nothing is shared between pharmacies or across associations.
- Every action is on the record, so the service you offer is defensible and
  accountable.

**Bottom line:** the data your pharmacies hand to the service stays *their* data.
SiroQ keeps it separate, private, and governed by rules you control — and it can
prove it, with a permanent journal.
